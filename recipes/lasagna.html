Description

This lasagna is a classic Italian dish that is made with layers of pasta, sauce, and cheese. It is a hearty and comforting meal that is perfect for a family dinner.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound lasagna noodles
  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 can of tomato sauce
  • 1 can of tomato paste
  • 2 cans of crushed tomatoes
  • 2 cans of tomato sauce

Steps

  1. Step 1: Cook the lasagna noodles in a large pot of boiling water for 8 to 10 minutes. Drain the noodles and rinse with cold water.
  2. Step 2: In a large pot, cook the sausage, ground beef, onion, and garlic over medium heat until the meat is browned and the onion is transparent.
  3. Step 3: Stir in the tomato sauce, crushed tomatoes, and tomato paste until the sauce is heated through.
  4. Step 4: Add the sugar, 2 teaspoons of salt, and 1 teaspoon of pepper to the sauce.
  5. Step 5: Simmer the sauce for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  6. Step 6: Preheat the oven to 375 degrees F (190 degrees C).
  7. Step 7: In a large bowl, mix together the egg, ricotta, and parsley until the mixture is smooth.
  8. Step 8: Spread 1 1/2 cups of sauce on the bottom of a 9x13 inch baking dish. Layer 1/2 each, lasagna noodles, ricotta mix, sauce, and Parmesan cheese. Repeat layering, and top with the remaining 2 cups of sauce.
  9. Step 9: Bake in the preheated oven for 50 minutes, or until the noodles are tender and the cheese is melted and bubbly.
  10. Step 10: Let the lasagna rest for 15 minutes before serving.
